# Pickers

A picker is a menu that lists items and filters them as you type: type a few
letters, press `Enter` to choose, and `Esc` to cancel. The pickers follow the
active theme without config changes.

## Open programs with the launcher

Press `$mod+d`, type part of a name, and press `Enter`. thinkpad runs fuzzel;
optiplex runs rofi.

## Answer confirmation menus

The power shortcuts reuse the same picker as a flat list of choices: yes and no,
one entry each. `Enter` confirms, `Esc` cancels, and pressing the shortcut again
while its menu is open dismisses it without acting.

## What is not here

Clipboard history and notification history pickers are not part of the current
setup; the launcher and the confirmation menus are the whole picker surface.
